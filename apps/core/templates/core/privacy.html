Privacy Notice

Last updated: February 1, 2026

Your Privacy Matters. This notice explains how ShopVault collects, uses, and protects your personal data. We are committed to transparency and giving you control over your information.
Table of Contents 1. Information We Collect 2. How We Use Your Information 3. Information Sharing 4. Cookies & Tracking 5. Data Security 6. Your Rights 7. Contact Us

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, including:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, password
  • Order Information: Shipping address, payment method, purchase history
  • Communication: Reviews, customer service inquiries, feedback

We also automatically collect:

  • Device and browser information
  • IP address and general location
  • Pages visited and products viewed
  • Search queries within ShopVault

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Process and deliver your orders
  • Provide customer support
  • Personalize your shopping experience
  • Send order updates and relevant communications
  • Prevent fraud and ensure security
  • Improve our services and develop new features

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service Providers: Companies that help us deliver services (payment processors, shipping carriers)
  • Sellers: Order details necessary to fulfill your purchases
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights

4. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, keep you logged in, and understand how you use ShopVault. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption, secure data storage, and regular security audits. While no system is completely secure, we work hard to protect your information.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and data
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Opt out of marketing communications

7. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact our Data Protection team at privacy@shopvault.com.
